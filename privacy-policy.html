Reliquary: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 October 2026 · Voyge Interactive
In short: Reliquary collects no personal information. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no in-app purchases, and it does not connect to the internet while you play. Your game progress stays on your device.

Who we are

Reliquary is a puzzle game made by Voyge Interactive ("we"). This policy explains what happens to information when you use the Reliquary app for Android.

Information we collect

None. We do not collect, store on our servers, share or sell any personal information, and we do not ask for any. There is no sign-in, no account, no chat, no social features, no advertising, no analytics or crash-reporting service, no location access and no in-app purchases.

Game data on your device

So that you can carry on where you left off, the game saves a small amount of game data only on your device:

  • which levels you have finished and the relics in your Cabinet;
  • your lives and when the next one comes back, and your power-ups;
  • your settings (sound, music, vibration, calm effects, bold shapes, left-handed, break reminders and daily play time) and today's play time.

This data never leaves your device through the game, and we cannot see it. Uninstalling the game, or clearing its storage in Android Settings, deletes it.

Android backup

If you use Android's own backup, Android may include this game data in your Google account backup so your progress can move to a new phone. That backup is provided and controlled by Google and by you, not by us, and we cannot access it. You can turn it off in your phone's settings.

Permissions

  • Vibration: for small "buzz" effects when you find a relic. You can turn it off in the game's Settings.
  • Internet: included by the app framework that runs the game. Reliquary itself makes no network requests.

Children

Reliquary is suitable for children and is designed for players aged 6 and up. Because the game collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children. It contains no ads, no purchases and no way to talk to other people. The only link that leaves the game is to this privacy policy, in Settings, behind a grown-ups check (press and hold, then answer a sum). We comply with the Google Play Families Policy and, because no personal information is collected, with the requirements of COPPA (US) and the GDPR (EU/UK) that apply to children's apps.

Your rights

As we hold no personal information about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct, export or delete. Your on-device game data is under your control and can be deleted at any time by uninstalling the game.

Changes to this policy

If we change how the game handles information, we will update this page and the date at the top. If a future version ever needs to collect information, we will explain it here and ask for any consent the law requires before it does.

Reliquary

A cosy puzzle game. Every level is a gem sculpture of an everyday thing with a golden relic hidden inside. Pick the right tools in the right order, dig your way in, and fill your museum Cabinet.

  • Collects no personal information. Plays offline.
  • Made for players aged 6 and up.
